COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the zero-wait digital appointment pass work?

When you confirm a slot on ApoCare, our system syncs directly with the doctor's live clinic schedule. You receive an instant digital QR pass. Present this at the clinic desk for priority admission without standing in registration queues.

Are all listed doctors verified by medical councils?

Yes. Every healthcare practitioner on ApoCare undergoes rigorous verification of their West Bengal Medical Council (WBMC) or National Medical Commission (NMC) registration credentials before being approved.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my consultation?

You can cancel anytime directly from your Patient Dashboard with 1-click. Full refunds are instantly processed back to your original payment mode for cancellations made at least 2 hours before the appointment.
